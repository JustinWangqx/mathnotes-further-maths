## Choose the method from the structure

An integrand containing a square root may simplify under a trigonometric or hyperbolic substitution. Products can suggest integration by parts. Powers indexed by $n$ often suggest a recurrence linking one integral to another.

Write the substitution, differential and limits explicitly. For an indefinite integral, return to the original variable and include the integration constant.

## Substitution patterns

- $\sqrt{a^2-x^2}$ often suggests $x=a\sin\theta$.
- $\sqrt{a^2+x^2}$ often suggests $x=a\sinh u$ or $x=a\tan\theta$.
- $\sqrt{x^2-a^2}$ can suggest $x=a\cosh u$ on $x\ge a>0$.

The interval matters: $\sqrt{v^2}=|v|$, not automatically $v$.

## Example 1: derive a reduction formula

Let $I_n=\int_0^{\pi/2}\sin^n x\,dx$. For integers $n\ge2$, integrate $\sin^{n-1}x\sin x$ by parts:

$$I_n=(n-1)\int_0^{\pi/2}\sin^{n-2}x\cos^2x\,dx.$$

The boundary term is zero. Using $\cos^2x=1-\sin^2x$ gives

$$I_n=(n-1)(I_{n-2}-I_n),\qquad \boxed{I_n=\frac{n-1}{n}I_{n-2}.}$$

The base values are $I_0=\pi/2$ and $I_1=1$. Hence $I_4=(3/4)(1/2)(\pi/2)=3\pi/16$.

## Polar integration

For a polar curve $r=r(\theta)$ with an interval tracing the region once, the area swept out is

$$A=\frac12\int_\alpha^\beta r^2\,d\theta.$$

The arc length is

$$L=\int_\alpha^\beta\sqrt{r^2+\left(\frac{dr}{d\theta}\right)^2}\,d\theta.$$

These methods appear in the supplied integration material. Check how the curve is traced before choosing limits or multiplying a sector by symmetry.

## Example 2: the area of a polar loop

For $r=1+\cos\theta$, find the area enclosed as $0\le\theta\le2\pi$. This interval traces the cardioid once.

$$A=\frac12\int_0^{2\pi}(1+2\cos\theta+\cos^2\theta)\,d\theta.$$

The three integrals inside the bracket are $2\pi$, $0$ and $\pi$, so $A=3\pi/2$.

## Common mistakes

Do not lose the boundary term in integration by parts without evaluating it, apply a recurrence outside its allowed values of $n$, or use $r$ instead of $r^2$ in a polar area. A final positive area or length is a useful check, but it does not replace correct limits.
