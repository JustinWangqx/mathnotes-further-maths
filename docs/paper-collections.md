The original `NO. 1`, `NO. 2`, … numbering stays inside each PDF. Paper/session/question references and examiner commentary in the supplied answer collections are retained. The collections are topic compilations of past-paper questions, rather than newly assembled full examination papers.

The additional Roots collection and Summation of series collection have no separately supplied answer file. Their reader pages say so. The manifest records filenames, page counts, sizes and SHA-256 checksums.

The reader displays lossless full-page WebP previews in original order. These are rendered from the unchanged question PDFs without cropping. The complete vector PDFs remain available for zoom, selectable text and download.
